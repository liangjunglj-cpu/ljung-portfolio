COLOPHON

HOW THIS WAS BUILT
THE FABRIC

Static HTML on GitHub Pages — no framework, no build step. Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono on a polycarbonate panel system. Cross-document View Transitions morph the rooms into each other; every page carries its own arcade layer.

THE GAMES

The walkable museum and the playable Ginkgo world are three.js first-person builds — pointer lock, stepped-floor collision, per-zone fog and light. The Ginkgo world's plan was solved by the real compiler; the browser only realizes it.

THE PASSPORT

Rooms, stations and secrets live in your browser's localStorage and nowhere else. Nothing is tracked, nothing leaves your machine. Clear it and the sixth room politely ceases to exist.

THE HANDS

Designed and directed by Chen Liang Jung; built in collaboration with Claude Code. The drawings, models, worlds and tools it documents are the real work — this site is just their playable index.
