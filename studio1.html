EX.04 · CAPSTONE

MoMA SINGAPORE

THE PADANGINVERTED PYRAMIDHUMAN × MACHINE CREATIVITY

A Center for Human Creativity + Machine Intelligence at the Padang — an inverted pyramid that withdraws beneath the civic ground. Visitors don't view finished work; they descend to make it, in a "vivarium for creative systems" where machines, handlers and visitors produce art together. A name-registry wall meets you at the threshold; the descent edge has no handrail — the absence is the argument.

GLANCE → LINGER → DEEP DIVE · oculus over the forum · authorship = designing the conditions under which the work can appear

"IF A MACHINE CAN DREAM ABOUT TWO CENTURIES OF HUMAN ART — AND THREE MILLION PEOPLE STAND THERE FOR 38 MINUTES — WHAT EXACTLY IS LEFT FOR THE HUMAN ARTIST TO DO?" · the process behind this project is written up in THE DREAM ENGINE →

EX.02

YARA MINGA

GREAT BARRIER REEFARCHITECTURE AS SUBTRACTIONGH + WASP

A reef habitat carved into inert seabed limestone near Lizard Island — architecture as geomorphological editing, with a non-human client: juvenile parrotfish and surgeonfish, whose grazing stabilises algae and lets coral recover. Five spatial types (chambers, curved connectors, graded porosity, light-calibrated cavities, grazing surfaces) grown as a rule-based aggregation in Grasshopper + Wasp — the seed of the later wasp-mcp work.

REFUGE → MOVEMENT → FEEDING · edit the terrain, let marine life re-author it

EX.03

SCARLET CLOISTER

PERSONA → DWELLINGCIRCLE · SQUARE · TRIANGLE

A dwelling generated from pure composition: nine black-and-white figure-ground studies of circle, square and triangle, anchored to a written persona of "exuberance, curiosity and theatrical optimism," then extruded into a 1:100 house — rooms turned inward around a planted cloister, matching the persona's public bluster and private conviction.

ABSTRACTION → INHABITATION · geometry first, program second

EX.01

LOOPSCAPE

GROUND AS DATUMONE MOVE, REPEATED

An inhabitable public landscape built from one repeatable operation — a concrete band that loops out of the ground and back into it. Where loops lift they become canopies and benches; where they touch down, paths and planted beds. Architecture and landscape as the same continuous material.

PROGRAM BY INFLECTION · conditions, not rooms
