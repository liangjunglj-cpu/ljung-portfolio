01 THE CITY, MOVING

unreal 5.8 · camera passes through the neon front

A third-person action RPG in the gacha mould: fast gunplay, dodges and spells across a Y2K-maximalist megacity split between two war fronts. Every character and every block of the city is original.

What makes it a portfolio piece rather than a hobby: the whole game is produced through a tool pipeline driven from Claude Code sessions. Characters go from sheet to rigged mesh; the city is grown by seeded generators through Almond's Rhino bridge; Unreal is built, tested and packaged by scripts that must return evidence.

01 · CHARACTERS

Sheet to rigged mesh

Each operative starts as a character sheet and a four-view turnaround, becomes a textured mesh in Meshy, is remeshed to game resolution, rigged, and retargeted onto Unreal's locomotion. Kohaku was first through the whole chain.

02 · WORLD

A city from seeded generators

The first Neon Front was 4,398 objects grown from one seed by typology generators talking to Rhino through Almond's bridge, exported layer by layer, lit in Blender, then rebuilt in Unreal. The districts since are assembled the same way: by script, never by hand.

03 · GAME

A C++ gameplay plugin

Pistol locomotion, dodge with perfect-dodge timing, three spells, enemy classes, a 16-lesson training course, cutscenes, and a sky full of kinetic traffic — airships, ad balloons and billboard drones on swept-clear paths.

04 · EVIDENCE

Nothing ships on trust

Each release must pass native tests, a UI regression run of 233 screenshot checks, and smoke routes through the packaged build. Playtest feedback is logged, traced to a cause, fixed, and re-captured before and after.
